Our Story

Founded in 2015 by pastry chef Maya Patel, Bloom & Bake started as a small kitchen in a cozy loft. We bake with locally sourced flour, butter, and a sprinkle of community spirit. Every loaf, cookie, and cake is made with love for families and friends.
